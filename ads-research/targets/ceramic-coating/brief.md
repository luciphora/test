# Ceramic coating — what's working, 11 Sep 2026

1,421 ads from 20 advertisers, 809 transcripts. Per-page Meta ad-library
pulls via Foreplay. One snapshot: a state, not a trend.

## The three-line version

1. **The offer is the product.** Anchor high, halve it, state the saving in dollars, then add a free
   *adjacent* service with its own dollar value. Never discount twice.
2. **The city goes in the first second.** Geo call-out is the biggest hook family in the category and in
   the last 60 days' launches (181 of 491).
3. **Nobody retargets.** 0 dynamic ads in 873 service ads. That is the opening.

## Segments — never pool these

| Segment | Ads | Live | What it sells |
|---|--:|--:|---|
| Service | 873 | 57 | an appointment |
| Product | 406 | — | a bottle |
| Franchise | 142 | — | a territory |

Everything below is the **service** segment.

## The offer, dismantled

- **163 of 192** ads naming a discount use exactly **50% off**.
- The percentage is converted to dollars. The two most-repeated figures in the whole category are a
  **saving ($1,300)** and the **value of free add-ons ($595)** — the price ($995) is only third.
- The bonus is always a *different service*: free windshield coating (335 ads), free interior
  detail (292). The core price never moves.
- The bonus is **gated behind watch-time**: *"since you watched the end of this video, we're throwing in
  a free windshield ceramic coating."* Lifts completion and makes the bonus feel earned.

## Top of funnel

- 480 of 491 service ads launched in the last 60 days (98%) **never leave Meta** —
  Messenger or an instant form, no landing page.
- Only 37 service ads (4%) use a dedicated capture page, and all of them are dead.
- Overall 83% of service ads are on-platform.

## Retargeting — the gap

Ad libraries never publish audience targeting, so it can only be inferred from format or from copy
addressed to a warm audience. Both checked:

- **14** dynamic product ads in the whole corpus, **0** in service.
- Every warm-audience copy hit was a false positive. "Since you watched the end of this video" is a
  bonus reveal aimed at a cold viewer.

A $995–$2,295 considered purchase with no retargeting layer anywhere in the category.

## Read the medians carefully

Median live tenure is **4 days**; the deepest live service ad is **373d**. Hook family does
**not** separate on survival here — the category churns creative far too fast, and several families rest
on one or two live ads. One advertiser, Huracán Nero, holds 351 of the 873 service ads,
46 of 57 live ones and 331 of the last 60 days' launches, so the medians
largely describe one Austin shop's testing cadence.

## What the data cannot say

No ad library publishes spend, impressions or conversions. Longevity means *not killed*, not profitable.
Dead-ad tenure is censored — Foreplay reports ~1 day for nearly every inactive ad — so all day-figures
come from live ads only. Foreplay's discovery index is unusable for ranking (a "ceramic coating" search
returned TikTok organic clips, duplicate records and a non-stick *cookware* ad) and overstates library
size by roughly half. A second pull in two weeks would turn this snapshot into a delta with real kill
dates.
