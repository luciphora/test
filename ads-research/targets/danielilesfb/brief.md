## What last week's verdict got wrong

On 3 Sep medical had held near 50% across two consecutive snapshots and I called it "a maintained position,
not a jackpot." The eight-week review halved it again to 11%. **Two readings at one level are not a floor;
they are two readings.** Treat any level confirmed fewer than three times as provisional.

The trades verdict is the one that has held all series: killed by week seven, never re-funded, unchanged
across four snapshots.

## The one thing he relaunched

Of the 4 new ads, one revives an offer he ran on 6 and 20 July and dropped — *"this is the best deal any
social media agency has ever put on the table. Sign with us this week, get an entire month of service
completely for free."* It is a revival, not a new offer. Reaching back for a retired lever is not what
scaling looks like.

## Limits

- **Longevity says "not killed", not "profitable".**
- Only the 266 snapshot-to-snapshot deaths have real tenure; the rest of the inactive corpus reports a
  bogus ~1 day.
- **The mass cull has at least three explanations** — a creative-generation refresh, a funnel consolidation,
  or an ad-account/billing change — and the Ad Library separates none of them. Do not report it as a
  performance signal.
- **7 Sep is missing from the activity chart**: the collector returned 0 active *and* 0 inactive, a data gap
  rather than a real zero. Dropped rather than plotted.
- ASR garbles brand terms ("MedSpot", "Rufus"). Quotes are directional.
- Coverage: everything live on any snapshot, plus everything started since 6 Jun 2026.

## What to take

1. **Don't copy this library right now.** The live set is a third of what it was and his proven creative is
   off. Wait for the next pull before treating anything here as a playbook.
2. **Trades remain the one durable no** — four snapshots, never re-funded.
3. **Expect scheduled reviews, not attrition.** His deaths cluster on exact tenures: 12, 43, 49, 57, 72 days.
4. **Watch what he relaunches, not what he kills.** The revived free-month offer tells you which lever he
   reaches for when he wants volume.
