The bottom line

What to take from this account

  • Open on the viewer, a stat, or what you replace, not on the service. His oldest live ad with a script ({{LONGEST_FIRST_DAYS}} days) opens on the agency he is replacing; the next two say "your competition isn't smarter than you" and "social media generates three times more leads than traditional advertising". When he does open on the offer, it is risk reversal: "a million views or you don't pay", "try our team for seven days".
  • Disarm your own guarantee. The {{DISARM_DAYS}}-day ad states "a million views or you don't pay", admits that is "just kind of what we say for marketing", then gives the real number. The scepticism becomes the reason to keep watching.
  • Build from the same five moves. Reframe (video beats ads, SEO and referrals), guarantee, how it works (you film 1–2 hours a month, we do the rest), proof, "click the link below". Most ads use two or three of them, not all five ({{EL_23_PCT}}% use two or three, {{EL_ALL5}} all five).
  • Swap the niche noun, keep the script. Since 17 Sep, {{NICHE_SWAP_ADS}} new ads open "If you're a …" with the niche dropped in: dentist, chiropractor, lawyer, medical professional.
  • Re-ship before you rewrite. Since 29 Sep he re-uploaded about {{SAME_REC_SRC}} existing recordings as {{SAME_REC}} new ads and wrote {{SCRIPTS_FRESH}} new scripts.
  • His current bet is {{TOPLAUNCH_FAM_LC}} openers. Since 29 Sep the most new ads ({{TOPLAUNCH_N}}) went to that hook family, and his most-uploaded script went up {{BET_TOP_N}} times.

For BYMOE: the hooks, the five moves and the niche swap transfer directly to local-service clients. The proof has to be your own: his 15.5m average and million-view guarantee are his claims, unverified, and not something to borrow. Longevity in the ad library means he kept paying for an ad, not that it made money.

Method and limits

What this data can and cannot say

{{PULL_ROWS}}
SnapshotOrderReturnedNew to unionUnion
  • No spend, no results. The Ad Library publishes whether an ad runs and since when. Longevity says "not killed", never "profitable", and a long run does not mean it was delivered every day.
  • Live pulls are sorted, partial samples. Each returns a different subset, so every union is a floor and absence is not death. Coverage depends on start date: the age-sorted pulls return the old ads in full and in a fixed order, and the newest-first pull reaches back only to the latest batch (most of September on 29 Sep, the 29 Sep launches on 10 Oct). Repeated files are counted once.
  • The history pull is a sample too. Asked for every ad started since 17 Sep, it returned {{HIST_N}} and still missed live ones, so every launch count here is a floor.
  • Foreplay re-keys ids and keeps stale twins. {{REKEYED_N}} of the 17 Sep proven ads now sit under new ids. One live DCO has a second record flagged inactive; Foreplay passes Meta ids through a double, so the two print a few digits apart. Ads are matched across snapshots by id, then Meta id, then start date with headline and script. {{BASE_IDS}} of the 17 Sep page's {{N_LIVE_17}} ads carry an id; the other {{BASE_NOID}} cannot be followed, except the proven DCOs, matched on start date.
  • Inactive flags are weak. They come from the history pull and {{PROBE_N}} small probes ({{PROBE_ADS}} records) of the proven ads' launch days. A dead ad's tenure is censored, and none is reported here.
  • Script matching. A new ad is a re-upload of an old recording when half or more of its five-word runs appear in one old ad (where both lengths are known, they match). It re-uses a script when it opens with the same 80 characters or shares 0.6 or more of its words with an old script (two unrelated old scripts typically share {{JAC_MED}}, and 99% share {{JAC_P99}} or less); 0.45–0.6 is a rewrite; below that it is new. {{NEW_TX}} of {{NEW_N}} new ads have a usable transcript; the rest cannot be checked. The old scripts on file are the 17 Sep set and later pulls, so "new" means new to those.
  • The presenter reading is visual judgement on small stills. That he is one person, or Daniel Iles, is inferred from appearance and first-person owner lines, not verified.
  • Transcripts are machine ASR. Quotes are close, not verbatim.
