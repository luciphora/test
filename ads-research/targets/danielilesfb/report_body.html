Method and limits

What this data can and cannot say

{{PULL_ROWS}}
SnapshotOrderReturnedNew to unionUnion
  • No spend, no results. The Ad Library publishes whether an ad runs and since when. Longevity says "not killed", never "profitable".
  • Live pulls are partial. Each returns a different date-sorted subset, so every union here is a floor. Absence from a pull is not death. The cull finding rests on a same-pull comparison: the pulls that return {{MID_PCT}}% of the established cohort return {{YOUNG_PCT_10}}% of the young one.
  • Age-sorted pulls agree on the old ads. All {{PREJUNE_PULLS_N}} longest-running and oldest-first pulls on 29 Sep and 10 Oct return the same {{PREJUNE_10}} live ads started before June.
  • Foreplay re-keys ids and keeps duplicates. Two of the 17 Sep proven ads now sit under new ids. At least one live DCO ad has a second record flagged inactive, its Meta id differing in the last digits. Ads are matched across snapshots by id, then Meta id, then start date with headline and script.
  • Inactive flags are weak. They come from the history pull and {{PROBE_N}} small probes ({{PROBE_ADS}} ads) of the proven set's launch windows. A dead ad's tenure is censored; none is reported here.
  • The daily active count is noisy and always below the union. It is used for shape only.
  • Script matching is on the opening words (the first 80 characters, normalised), so a re-shot ad that opens the same way counts as the same script.
  • Transcripts are machine ASR. Quotes are close, not verbatim.
