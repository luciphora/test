It isn't the creative — it's the audience

Method

How these ads were picked

Meta's Ad Library publishes no spend, impressions or conversions for commercial ads. Nobody can read performance out of it directly. Two signals are available, and both are inference:

  • Longevity. A performance advertiser does not pay to keep a losing ad live. The longer an ad runs, the more of his own culling it has survived.
  • Re-investment. Fresh ads pushed into a segment weeks after launch mean the first round paid back.

Everything featured here is ranked on those two, in that order. Nothing was chosen because it read well.

Limits — read before quoting any of this

  • Longevity is a proxy, not profit. A well-funded advertiser can keep a mediocre ad alive. Longevity says "not killed", not "profitable".
  • Stopped ads have no usable tenure. 98.4% of the 1,772 inactive ads report exactly one day, which is a data artifact rather than a real stop date. So this report never says how long a dead ad ran — only that it started and then stopped. Live-ad tenure is sound.
  • Wave two is six days old. It shows where he re-invested, which is the useful part. It cannot yet show whether wave two worked.
  • Transcripts are machine ASR and garble brand terms — "MedSpot" for med spa, "Rufus" for roofers. Quotes are directionally right, not verbatim.
  • Coverage: every currently-live ad regardless of age, plus every ad started since 6 June 2026. Inactive ads older than that are outside this pull.
  • 1,505 of 2,025 ads had usable audio; 885 of those classified into 22 hook families derived from this corpus. The remaining 620 are genuine one-offs.

If you take four things

What to do with it

  • Sell to licensed, high-ticket practices — not trades. He proved the same pitch converts in one and not the other, then backed it with a second budget.
  • Open on the status quo, not the guarantee. 60% survival versus 30%.
  • One script, N verticals. Swap the noun, re-shoot, let survival pick the winner. The production cost of testing a vertical is one afternoon.
  • Re-pull in three weeks. 46 fresh medical ads landed 19 August. Whichever clear 60 days are the next control set.
