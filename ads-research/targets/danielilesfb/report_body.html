Method and limits

What this data can and cannot say

{{PULL_ROWS}}
SnapshotOrderReturnedNew to unionUnion
  • No spend, no results. The Ad Library publishes whether an ad runs and since when. Longevity says "not killed", never "profitable", and a long run does not mean it was delivered every day.
  • Live pulls are sorted, partial samples. Each returns a different subset, so every union is a floor and absence is not death. Coverage depends on start date: the age-sorted pulls return the old ads in full and in a fixed order, and the newest-first pull covers only the last fortnight. Repeated files are counted once.
  • The history pull is a sample too. Asked for every ad started since 17 Sep, it returned {{HIST_N}} and still missed live ones, so every launch count here is a floor.
  • Foreplay re-keys ids and keeps stale twins. Two of the 17 Sep proven ads now sit under new ids. One live DCO has a second record flagged inactive; Foreplay passes Meta ids through a double, so the two print a few digits apart. Ads are matched across snapshots by id, then Meta id, then start date with headline and script. {{BASE_IDS}} of the 17 Sep page's {{N_LIVE_17}} ads carry an id; the other {{BASE_NOID}} cannot be followed, except the proven DCOs, matched on start date.
  • Inactive flags are weak. They come from the history pull and {{PROBE_N}} small probes ({{PROBE_ADS}} records) of the proven ads' launch days. A dead ad's tenure is censored, and none is reported here.
  • Script matching uses three tests: same opening (first 80 characters), same words (token overlap of 0.8 or more on the whole script), and same recording (half or more of the new ad's five-word runs found in one old ad). {{NEW_TX}} of {{NEW_N}} new ads have a usable transcript; the rest cannot be checked.
  • The presenter reading is visual judgement on small stills. That he is one person, or Daniel Iles, is inferred from appearance and first-person owner lines, not verified.
  • Transcripts are machine ASR. Quotes are close, not verbatim.
