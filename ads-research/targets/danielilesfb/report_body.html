Method

How these ads were picked

Meta's Ad Library publishes no spend, impressions or conversions for commercial ads. Two signals are available, and both are inference:

  • Longevity. A performance advertiser does not pay to keep a losing ad live. The longer an ad runs, the more of his own culling it has survived.
  • Where new money goes. Fresh ads pushed into a segment mean the last round earned another look; a segment skipped in a new wave is one he's stopped believing in.

Everything featured here is ranked on those two. Nothing was chosen because it read well.

Limits — read before quoting any of this

  • Longevity is a proxy, not profit. A well-funded advertiser can keep a mediocre ad alive. Longevity says "not killed", not "profitable".
  • Most stopped ads have no usable tenure. 98% of the historical inactive ads report exactly one day, a data artifact. The only dead ads with a real tenure are the {{N_DIED_ALL}} that died between snapshots — and those are the ones this report leans on.
  • Two measures of "live" disagree. Foreplay's daily active count (the chart) and its per-ad live flag (every table) don't reconcile — the chart reads 86 on 2 Sep while the flag returns {{N_LIVE}}. The chart is used for shape only; every number in a table comes from the per-ad flag.
  • A verdict from two snapshots is fragile — this series has now proved it. "Medical held" rested on two readings at ~50%; the next pull halved it. Treat any level confirmed fewer than three times as provisional.
  • The mass cull has at least three explanations and the Ad Library separates none of them: a creative refresh, a funnel consolidation, or an ad-account change. Do not report it as a performance signal.
  • 7 Sep is missing from the activity chart — the collector returned 0 active and 0 inactive, a gap rather than a real zero. It is dropped rather than plotted.
  • Transcripts are machine ASR and garble brand terms — "MedSpot" for med spa, "Rufus" for roofers. Quotes are directionally right, not verbatim.
  • Coverage: every ad live on any snapshot regardless of age, plus every ad started since 6 June 2026. Inactive ads older than that are outside this pull.

If you take four things

What to do with it

  • Don't copy this library right now. Whatever is happening — refresh, consolidation or account change — the live set is a sixth of what it was and his proven creative is switched off. Wait for the next pull before treating anything here as a working playbook.
  • Trades remain the one durable no. Killed by week seven, never re-funded, unchanged across four snapshots. That is a verdict confirmed enough times to trust.
  • Expect a scheduled review, not gradual attrition. His deaths cluster on exact tenures — 12, 43, 49, 57, 72 days. If you run a book this size, put the review on the calendar and cut in batches.
  • Watch what he relaunches, not what he kills. The single most informative ad this window is the revived free-month offer: it tells you which lever he reaches for when he wants volume.
