## Bottom line

**One promise, a few durable hooks, re-shipped at volume.** He sells done-for-you short-form video to business
owners. His ads sell one promise two ways: "Our avg clients get 15.5m views" in the headline ({{H155_PCT}}% of
{{N_LIVE}} live ads), and a views-or-you-don't-pay guarantee inside the older scripts. His oldest live ad is
{{MAX_LIVE}} days; {{P180_10}} live ads are past 180 days.

1. **Open on the viewer, a stat, or what you replace, not on the service.** {{LONGEST_NONOFFER}} of his
   {{LONGEST_N}} longest-running scripted ads open on something other than the offer. The two longest seen live on
   17 Sep and today open "your competition isn't smarter than you" ({{LG_446}} days) and "social media generates
   three times more leads than traditional advertising" ({{LG_435}} days). When a long runner opens on the offer, it
   is risk reversal: "a million views or you don't even pay", "try our team for seven days".
2. **Disarm your own guarantee.** The {{DISARM_DAYS}}-day ad states the million-view guarantee, admits it is "just
   kind of what we say for marketing", then gives what he says his average client really gets (unverified). Copy
   the structure only with a guarantee you will honour and a number you can show.
3. **Build from the same five moves.** Reframe, risk reversal, how it works, proof, "click the link below". Most ads
   use two or three ({{EL_23_PCT}}%); {{EL_ALL5}} use all five.
4. **Swap the niche noun, keep the script.** Since 17 Sep, {{NICHE_SWAP_ADS}} new ads open "If you're a …" with a
   trade dropped in. The move has lasted: {{NICHE_IF_N}} older ads opening "If you're a …" for {{NICHE_IF_NOUNS}}
   are live at {{NICHE_IF_MIN}}–{{NICHE_IF_MAX}} days.
5. **He fills batches with re-uploads; that is habit, not proof.** {{SAME_REC}} of {{NEW_TX}} transcribed new ads
   re-use about {{SAME_REC_SRC}} earlier recordings, all {{SAME_REC_SRC_ADS}} sources under 30 days old on
   17 Sep and {{SAME_REC_SRC_LIVE}} still live. Only {{SCRIPTS_FRESH}} scripts are new.
6. **Where the new volume went: client case studies.** {{TOPLAUNCH_N}} ads since 29 Sep, all re-uploads of
   {{CS_STORIES}} client stories; {{CS_SRC_LIVE}} of the {{CS_SRC_N}} originals still show live. Volume, not a proven
   winner.

For BYMOE: the reframe, how it works and the click-the-link close transfer as they are, and so do the openers and
the niche swap. Risk reversal and proof need a guarantee you will honour and client numbers you can document. His
15.5m average, guarantee, stats and client results are his claims, unverified. Longevity means an ad is still
running; it says nothing about spend or results.
