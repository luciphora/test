# Certified Safe — competitor ad scan, 2026-09-16

1,149 ads from 21 advertisers across duct cleaning, carpet cleaning,
water damage and mold. 330 transcripts. Per-page Meta ad-library pulls via Foreplay.
One snapshot: a state, not a trend. **Your own ads are not in this** — Certified Safe is not indexed by
Foreplay, so this is what the category does, not what you are doing differently.

## The headline

campaign covering all five services underperforms.

## The structural difference

**439 of 746 duct/carpet ads (59%) land on a quote or booking page.
7 of 315 restoration ads do (2%).**

Stanley Steemer sends every ad to `/cleaning-quote-pricing`. Zerorez to a city-scoped
`/schedule-service-online`. Restoration advertisers point at a homepage. That is the clearest operational
gap in the scan.

## The offer

The entry-price ladder is tight and public: **$99 / $119 / $129 / $149 / $159 / $199 / $249**, usually
gated to first-time customers (11 ads). Unlike most local-service categories the
biggest number *is* the price, not a discount.

**$99 has become the number the category argues with** — 29 mentions are premium operators naming it as
what they are *not*: *"real service — not a $99 cheap sweep"*. You do not have to beat $99; you have to
make it the wrong question.

Themes that transfer, counted across 1061 service ads:

- **Guarantee (23)** — "most thorough cleaning or it's free" carries the five longest-running live ads.
- **Cost reframe (32)** — the strongest duct angle is the energy bill, not health. Your site leads on 99.7% particulate reduction; the market leads on the bill.
- **Replace vs restore (28)** — "before you replace, let us reset" anchors a $149 clean against a four-figure replacement.
- **Referral partner (23)** — 1-800 WATER DAMAGE barely advertises to homeowners; it advertises to plumbers, property managers and insurance adjusters, asking for the referral.

## What I'd do

1. **Split by buying mode, not by service list.** Duct + carpet + dryer vent: one campaign, gated entry price, booking page. Mold and water damage should stop competing for that budget.
2. **Build the booking page.** 59% vs 2% is the single clearest difference between what works and what doesn't.
3. **Lead ducts on the energy bill.**
4. **Put a guarantee where your discount is.**
5. **For mold and water damage, advertise to referrers** — plumbers, property managers, adjusters. In a Boston radius that is a countable audience; panicking homeowners are not.

## Limits

No ad library publishes spend, impressions or conversions. Longevity means *not killed*, not profitable.
Dead-ad tenure is censored, so all day-figures come from live ads only. Local Boston rivals are effectively
invisible in Foreplay — a "Boston restoration" search returned a seed company and a dog bakery — so these
are national operators running the same plays at scale, not the firms bidding against you on Route 1.
One advertiser (Renew Carpet Cleaning) is Irish and flagged as such. Send a Meta Ad Library link for your
own page and the next pass becomes a direct side-by-side.
