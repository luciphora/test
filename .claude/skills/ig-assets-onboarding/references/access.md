# Instagram access — how to request it safely

Menu names in Meta's tools change frequently. If a step below doesn't match what the client sees, search Meta Business Help Center for "add a partner to your business portfolio" and follow the current wording.

## Preferred: partner access via Business Portfolio (Business accounts)

Works when the client's IG is a professional account connected to a Meta Business Portfolio (formerly Business Manager).

1. Get the agency's **Business Portfolio ID** (Business settings → Business info) and give it to the client.
2. Client: Business settings → Users → **Partners** → Add → "Give a partner access to your assets" → enter the agency's portfolio ID.
3. Client selects assets and roles:
   - **Instagram account** — content publishing, messages/comments (if community management is in scope), insights
   - **Facebook Page** linked to the IG — only if posting cross-platform or running ads
   - **Ad account** — only if ads are in scope; prefer "Advertiser/manage campaigns" over full admin
   - **Pixel / dataset** — only for ads
4. Agency: assign the shared assets to the specific team members who need them.
5. Log in the tracker: asset, role, granted by, date. No secrets.

Ask for the narrowest role that covers the scope. Full admin control isn't needed to post, and asking for less builds trust.

## Account is not set up for this yet

- **Personal account** → client switches to Professional (Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account). Business vs Creator: Business if they sell products/services under a brand; Creator for personal-brand accounts. Both work with Meta Business Suite.
- **No Facebook Page / Business Portfolio** → walk the client through creating a Business Portfolio and connecting the IG account, or have them add an agency team member directly to the IG account from Meta Business Suite if a full portfolio is overkill.

## Last resort: shared login

Only when partner access is truly impossible.

- Share via a password manager (1Password, Bitwarden, etc.) shared vault or one-time share link. Never in chat, email, SMS, Notion, docs, or the tracker.
- 2FA stays on. Set it up so the client keeps the authenticator or adds the agency's shared authenticator — never disable 2FA "to make it easier".
- Expect Instagram to flag new-location logins; warn the client they may need to approve a "Was this you?" prompt.

## Other access to collect (if used)

- Scheduler (Postiz, Later, Planoly, Meta Business Suite planner) — connect the IG channel; record which tool and who connected it
- Link-in-bio tool (Linktree, Beacons, Stan, own site page) — editor access
- Design tools — Canva brand kit / Figma file share
- Website/landing page analytics or UTM convention — if reporting on link clicks

## Tracker entries — what to record

| What | Role / level | Method | Granted by | Date | Status |
|------|--------------|--------|-----------|------|--------|
| IG @handle | Content + insights | BP partner | client name | YYYY-MM-DD | ✅ |
| Ad account | Advertiser | BP partner | client name | — | ❌ requested |
| Postiz | Channel connected | OAuth | team member | YYYY-MM-DD | ✅ |

Never put passwords, recovery codes, or 2FA seeds in this table.
